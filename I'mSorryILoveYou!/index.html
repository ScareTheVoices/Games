Create Your Student

How to Play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Walk up to a classmate and press E (or click them) to talk.

Each day moves through Morning, Announcements, a Free Period, Club Activity, Going Home, and Night. Use the button in the top-right of the school screen to move to the next part of the day.

Open the pause menu (top-right, or press Esc) to check your character, relationships, evidence, the map, and save your game.

Talk to people, pay attention to where they are and aren't, and start forming your own theory about who — and what — is really going on at this school.
