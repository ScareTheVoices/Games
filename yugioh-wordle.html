How to play
  • One free hint is revealed at the start. You have 5 hints total and 6 guesses.
  • Click any unrevealed hint (or “Use Random Hint”) to spend a hint and narrow the possible cards.
  • Guess by card name. Each guess compares Frame, Level/Rank/Link, Attribute, Type, ATK & DEF.
  • Green = exact match · Yellow = close on numbers (within 500 ATK/DEF or ±1 Level) · Red = miss.
  • Only monster cards are in the pool.
