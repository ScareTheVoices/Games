How to play
  • One free hint is revealed at the start. You have 5 hints total and 6 guesses.
  • Tap any unrevealed hint (or Random Hint) to narrow the possible cards.
  • Open the Card Catalogue to browse and pick a monster to guess — no need to know names by heart.
  • The catalogue defaults to cards that still match your hints. Toggle “All cards” to see everything.
  • Green = exact · Yellow = close on numbers · Red = miss (+ arrows for higher/lower).
